BEEPSTRAP PARADOX
-----------------

Introduction:
How did Dr Beep get 150 ideas to make all this games?
This game will reveal it. He gets his ideas from his future self who
plants the found idea on your path to find it. When you have placed the idea
you need to get the next idea.

The gameplay:
After loading the game you press NEWLINE to start a game.
You start at the left top corner facing down (D).
Other views are left (L), up (U) or right (R).
You need to pick up the idea (?) and then move to one of the two timemachines to go
back in time to place the idea on the same place you found it for your earlier version to find it.

Easy, right!
Not so fast!
In the first run you did not see anybody. So when you go back in time to place your idea 
you may never be in sight of your previous self or you create a paradox.
The path you took in the first run is recorded and played back by the computer.
You need to place the idea on the "." without being seen and before the recorded play gets to the field.
If you fail to place the idea on the destination you create a paradox and you lose a life.

How many ideas can you place in the head of Dr Beep?
You need to complete a run to a timemachine before the timer reaches 0.

The following paradoxes can appear:
1) going to the timemachine without an idea.
2) not placing the idea on de destination.
3) earlier Dr Beep enters timemachine without an idea.
4) not on time at the timemachine to go back in time.
5) in sight of previous Dr Beep, he sees the future Dr Beep.

Extra option:
You don't need to wait for the time to reach zero. When you are at the timemachine
(and other too) you can end the time with S. You need to press it some time.

Controls:
Q move in direction shown
O rotate anticlockwise
P rotate clockwise
Z place idea
S stop timer
NewLine start (new) game
